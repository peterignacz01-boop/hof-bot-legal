Privacy Policy

Hall of Fame Bot · Last updated: 23 September 2026

1. Data the bot may process

When you use the bot, it may process information provided by Discord that is necessary to perform its function, including your Discord user ID, username or display name, server and channel identifiers, submission text, message metadata, and files or links you intentionally submit.

2. How the data is used

This information is used only to receive a Hall of Fame submission, identify who submitted it, format it, and publish it to the designated Hall of Fame channel.

3. Storage

The bot is not intended to maintain a separate permanent database of user submissions. Published Hall of Fame content is stored by Discord in the relevant server channels. Temporary technical logs may be created by the hosting environment for operation and troubleshooting.

4. Sharing and sale of data

The bot does not sell personal data and does not use personal data for advertising. Submitted content is shared only within the Discord server as part of the Hall of Fame feature, unless a user independently shares it elsewhere.

5. Data removal

If you want a Hall of Fame submission removed, contact a server administrator or the bot operator through the Discord server. Deleting a Hall of Fame message removes it from the server channel, subject to Discord's own data retention practices.

6. Third-party services

The bot operates through Discord and is therefore also subject to Discord's own privacy practices and terms. Hosting providers may process limited technical data required to run the bot.

7. Security

Reasonable steps are taken to limit the bot's permissions and protect its credentials. However, no online service can guarantee absolute security.

8. Changes

This policy may be updated if the bot's functionality or data handling changes. The current version will be published on this page.

9. Contact

For privacy questions or removal requests, contact the bot operator or a server administrator through the Discord server where the bot is installed.
